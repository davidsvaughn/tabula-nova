### Target and protocol

- Probability that **next-quarter real GDP growth is strictly negative**, using growth derived from RTDSM's `First` vintage column. This is not an NBER recession forecast, and not a claim to reproduce an original rounded BEA headline number exactly.
- Survey quarters 1992Q1–2024Q4; 132 potential events, **131 eligible**. Excluded 1995Q3 because its 1995Q4 target has a missing first-release value. No substitution from latest revised GDP.
- Evaluation: **84 survey quarters**, 2004Q1–2024Q4; targets 2004Q2–2025Q1; **10 contractions**. No missing evaluation labels.
- Features: contemporaneous mean RECESS1–5, next-quarter respondent p25/median/p75/standard deviation, response count. Ten features, one aggregate row per survey quarter.
- Three frozen seven-year blocks. For a block starting quarter S, training targets must be `<= S - 4`; this intentionally conservative embargo avoids near-boundary label use but is not a full exact-release-timestamp audit.
- Fixed baselines: direct mean RECESS2, training contraction frequency, regularized logistic calibration of logit(RECESS2), and histogram gradient boosting. No random split, after-score hyperparameter search, or replacement of the declared configuration.
- Brier and natural-log loss; log-loss probabilities clipped to `[1e-6, 1-1e-6]`. Same eligible events for every model.

The first fold has only one positive training event; later folds remain small and class-imbalanced. The quiet middle evaluation block has only one contraction. These counts sharply limit interpretation of both averages and apparent per-block improvements.

Do not attribute false alarms to model architecture or a particular feature without controlled ablations. Means-only / drop-response-count comparisons and a default-eight-estimator sensitivity would be explicitly post-result exploratory studies; none was run here, and none may replace these original results.

### Boundaries of the result

- This is retrospective feasibility, not a certified point-in-time or untouched confirmatory study. Today's cleaned historical archives may contain corrections.
- SPF does not require the particular `First` outcome vintage we chose; comparison is conditional on that explicit operationalization.
- The four-quarter embargo is conservative by target period, not proof of every exact release's availability. Publication dates are actual days; intraday release clocks were not reconstructed.
- Learned models are frozen for seven years at a time. This isolates a reproducible first comparison but is not the intended production retraining cadence.
- Quarter outcomes are serially correlated, contractions are scarce, and no confidence interval/significance claim is made.
- Two estimators are not the default eight-estimator configuration. Neither this negative result nor the positive middle block settles TabPFN's general macro usefulness.
- No LimiX, TS3, Fast, Thinking, full regression-distribution, or LLM-hybrid result is claimed.
