# LOG-003 — Revised research plan and initial judgment

Date: 2026-10-01. Read with [executed evidence](LOG-002.md) and [data inventory](../DATA-SOURCES.md). This is the next-stage plan, not a claim that the full research program has been executed.

## Bottom line

The chats identify a credible research direction: **pretrained tabular inference for small-sample economic probabilities and conditional forecast combination**. They do not establish a financial edge. Our existing data is rich enough to start serious work without buying a new broad market feed. Its main weaknesses are point-in-time semantics, independently resolved event counts, stopped collection windows, and missing archived economic consensus/nowcast features.

A useful positive surprise: Kalshi's historical tier already yields five years of monthly CPI event metadata. A useful negative result: TabPFN local inference is gated by explicit Prior Labs license authentication, despite ungated Hugging Face metadata. A useful baseline result: plain ridge beat boosted trees and volatility persistence in the small SPY pilot. Complexity must earn its place.

## Priority 1 — A release-level CPI benchmark

### Freeze the contract

One row per scheduled US headline CPI release. Target: **originally reported CPI-U, seasonally adjusted monthly percent change, rounded as published, strictly greater than 0.3%**. Not annual inflation, not core CPI, not unrounded index-derived inflation, and not an earnings-call mention of inflation.

Decision cutoff: **16:00 America/New_York on the preceding US federal business day**, using the actual BLS release calendar and a versioned holiday calendar. Store the exact UTC instant. This selects the previous available approximately 10am ET Cleveland Fed update; never use release-day nowcasts made after 08:30 ET. Keep missing/canceled releases explicitly missing; do not fill October 2025 with a nowcast and call it an official observation.

### Acquisition and reconciliation

1. Combine `KXCPI` historical and live tiers using the fetched cutoff; examine legacy `CPI` separately for coverage/rule changes. Preserve raw rules, thresholds, release month, result, open/close/settlement times. Deduplicate by stable market identity.
2. Reconcile each label with the original BLS press release. One June 2026 sample already matches. Store source URL, content hash and extraction fields; reject mismatches rather than assuming market settlement equals the proposed target.
3. Retrieve each exact-threshold contract's historical quotes around the cutoff. Only candles ending at/before cutoff are eligible. Keep bid, ask, timestamp, age and spread; no interpolation from future candles. Start with a predeclared maximum quote age of two hours and report the excluded sample; revise that rule only in an explicitly separate exploratory cohort.
4. Build lagged CPI features from releases/vintages available before cutoff, not today's revised series. Seek archived Cleveland Fed nowcasts next. Validate the historical export and publication clock before treating it as PIT-safe.
5. Obtain timestamped monthly consensus only from a documented historical source. Finnhub economic calendar currently returns 403; do not manufacture consensus from later news. A no-consensus economic/nowcast cohort remains valid but must be labelled as such, never presented as beating consensus.
6. Produce a coverage report before modelling: independent releases, missing labels, matched contracts, quote-age exclusions, nowcast coverage, consensus coverage, and counts by year/regime.

Minimal row schema:

`event_id, reference_month, release_at_utc, decision_at_utc, label_available_at_utc, target_value_initial, target_binary, source_url, source_hash, feature_available_at_utc (per feature), market_ticker, rules_hash, quote_at_utc, bid, ask, quote_age_seconds, nowcast_asof, consensus_asof, retrieval_at_utc`.

### Models and measurement

- Baselines: expanding historical frequency; market midpoint plus bid/ask probability envelope; regularized logistic regression; conservative boosted trees; nowcast/consensus converted to probability with **past-only forecast residuals**.
- Candidate engines: TabPFN-3.5; LimiX-2 once usage rights and its runtime are resolved. Pin checkpoint/code/inference configuration. Do not call Fast, Plus, Thinking and default interchangeable.
- Walk forward one release at a time after a minimum 24 resolved-release warmup. With only 53 currently found exact-threshold historical contracts, this initially leaves very few tests: exploratory evidence only. More thresholds do not fix the independent-sample problem.
- Score market-only, economics-only and economics-plus-forecasts on identical eligible dates. Report longer no-market cohorts separately. Generate any learned base forecast out of sample before stacking.
- Brier and log loss primary; reliability plots and paired release/block-bootstrap intervals. Stronger support requires gains surviving multiple chronological blocks and subsequent unseen releases, not a single favorable aggregate.
- Use the historical data for development, then freeze the pipeline and collect prospective predictions. This program needs months of new economic events; do not imply a one-session investigation can settle the edge question.

**Acceptance:** a provenance-complete release table, reproducible same-sample predictions for authorized models/baselines, leakage checks and explicit coverage/exclusion report. No trade recommendation.

## Priority 2 — Faster, larger event-conditioned equity benchmark

Use JevTrader's existing 2025/2026 event tables and already leak-fixed price-window conventions. Test whether foundation models add value beyond the existing sign/activity models and Jev features.

- First target: fixed-horizon post-news volatility/risk or `abs(ar_30) > 1%`, not next absolute stock price. Preserve the existing return units; the economic meaning of 1% must be stated as log-return or converted to simple-return threshold.
- Features: genuinely pre-event returns, volume, sector/market context and predeclared text-derived features. Exclude `vol_post30`, `vol_ratio_30`, `ar_30` and other future fields from predictors.
- Group related stories/tickers and overlapping reaction windows; block by date; do not randomly split 123k event rows.
- Archived Benzinga text and synthetic receipt clocks create a backfill-validity limit. Call this a retrospective content benchmark until an arrival-time-safe cohort is separately evaluated. Existing 2026 results have already been inspected in JevTrader, so 2026 is not a pristine final holdout.
- For earnings-specific experiments, add original report/filing acceptance times, pre-release consensus snapshots and sector-adjusted outcomes. A latest earnings calendar or current fundamental lookup is insufficient.

**Acceptance:** reproduce an existing simple baseline on a frozen event cohort, then compare engines without changing labels/universe after observing their scores.

## Priority 3 — Conditional Kalshi calibration on a recurring family

The JevTrader h24 cohort has 8,955 market rows but only 1,583 event tickers. Deduplicate the 97 repeated rows and audit disagreements. Choose one recurring mechanism/event family, not all politics/mentions/entertainment pooled indiscriminately.

Compare market-implied probability, logistic calibration, boosted calibration, TabPFN and LimiX using market probability/spread/age and only pre-outcome covariates. Split by chronological resolution/decision blocks while keeping each underlying event together; training labels must already have resolved at the prediction timestamp. Treat archived hindsight-generated LLM features separately from true archived forecasts. The 176-row alpha-claw paper ledger may help verify the latter, but requires an independent-event count and provenance audit.

This track can test conditional calibration with more observations than monthly CPI, but it is **not a substitute for the economic-release experiment**. Weather remains an attractive later recurring family only after historical forecast-vintage and station/settlement rules are acquired.

## Supporting track — Complete the volatility model comparison

The executed SPY pilot proves market access, chronological/purged construction and baselines. After the license prerequisite, run the exact same frozen script with TabPFN, then assess LimiX feasibility. Expand to intraday realized variance using existing SPY minute bars and compare HAR-RV/EWMA/GARCH before claiming anything beyond an engineering smoke result.

The current script uses only two TabPFN estimators, intentionally below the default eight; report that compute budget explicitly. Its log-variance target and QLIKE metric may favor different estimands; a fuller study should predeclare whether it targets conditional log variance, conditional mean variance, or a predictive distribution.

## Access and deployment gates

1. **TabPFN local run:** user completes https://ux.priorlabs.ai license review/acceptance and supplies `TABPFN_TOKEN`. No secret should be pasted into a log. Then rerun the stored pilot; no new market-data pull needed.
2. **LimiX:** confirm the custom license fits the intended research/commercial trajectory. Its explicit commercial-objective research restriction is stronger than simply “not live trading yet.” Local inference and performance remain untested.
3. **CPI features:** authoritative labels, historical nowcasts/consensus and feature-availability timestamps still need assembly. Public market history is now verified, not blocked.
4. **Trading deployment:** separate rights, calibration stability, executable prices/size, fees, spreads, slippage and market rules. Nothing in this initial investigation authorizes or supports a live position.
